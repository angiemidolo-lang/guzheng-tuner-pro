Privacy Policy

Guzheng Tuner Pro · 古筝调音 Pro

Effective date: September 24, 2026

Home Support

English

Guzheng Tuner Pro is designed to work without an account. It does not collect, store, or upload personal data.

Microphone

The microphone is used only to measure pitch while you tune. Audio is analyzed on this device. It is not recorded, saved, or sent to any server.

Settings

Language, A4 reference, and tolerance stay in this device’s local settings. They are not synced or uploaded.

Tracking

The app does not track you, does not show ads, and does not include analytics SDKs.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact information on the support page.

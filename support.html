Frequently asked questions

Why is there no sound?
Check the device volume and silent mode, then tap Play Reference again.

How do I pick a string?
Tap a string on the board, or use the numbered chips. String 1 is nearest your body (highest); string 21 is farthest (lowest).

Does the app upload my audio?
No. Pitch is measured on this device. Nothing is recorded or uploaded.

中文帮助

为什么没有声音？
请检查设备音量和静音模式，然后再次点击「播放参考音」。

如何选择弦？
点琴面上的弦，或用编号按钮。1 弦最近身体、音最高；21 弦最远、音最低。

会上传我的声音吗？
不会。音高只在本机测量，不会录制或上传。
